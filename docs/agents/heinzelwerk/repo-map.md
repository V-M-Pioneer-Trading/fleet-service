4. MONEY / RATE BUDGET:
- Every game call is `spaceTradersRequest` -> `${ST_GATEWAY_URL}/proxy/my/...`, spending the shared SpaceTraders budget at the gateway (client.ts:64-81, 13-17; src/config.ts:21-23); no direct SpaceTraders URL (CLAUDE.md:137-140). All 12 routes spend it; deliver also makes a second, non-game call to agent-service (contracts.controller.ts:22-29).
- Credit-affecting: `refuel` buys fuel at a market (ships.controller.ts:81-89); contract `deliver` (contracts.controller.ts:16-32). Ship/cargo purchases and cargo sells live in agent-service (README.md:22-24).
- No retries anywhere (README.md:257-259). Caller's `Authorization` forwarded verbatim to st-gateway for queue priority (client.ts:76; CLAUDE.md:51-56); no X-Priority sent (CLAUDE.md:56).
